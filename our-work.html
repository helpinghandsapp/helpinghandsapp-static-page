Our Work

Helping Hands Platform (Helping Hands) is a grassroots, youth-led not-for-profit organization that works to increase youth community engagement! Helping Hands aims to help young people build their skills and careers through volunteer, entrepreneurship, and early career exploration.

Our Services

  • Free customized volunteer matching services and 1-on-1 volunteer application help
  • Free public workshops with a focus on volunteering, career exploration, capacity-building, and leadership skills. *We charge a fee for workshop requests from schools and community organizations to cover some of our workshop expenses. Email us at community@helpinghandsapp.com and we can send you a quote.
  • Other in-person or virtual volunteer and leadership focused programming (Ex. Mentorship, Youth Action Committee, Volunteer Fairs, and more)

Our History

Helping Hands began with Janelle Hinds. While in high school, she noticed many students struggled to choose career paths and complete their community service hours.

To make volunteering more accessible, she created the Helping Hands App, connecting youth with volunteer opportunities and helping them track their hours (*currently in alpha and unavailable). Since becoming a non-profit in 2019, Helping Hands has expanded to offer mentorship, workshops, and community programs that empower youth.
